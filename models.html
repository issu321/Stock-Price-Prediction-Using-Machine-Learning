Metrics

Evaluation Criteria

📊

R² Score

Coefficient of determination. Measures how much variance in stock prices is explained by the model. 1.0 = perfect prediction.

R² = 1 − (SS_res / SS_tot)
📏

MAE

Mean Absolute Error. Average dollar difference between predicted and actual prices. Lower is better.

MAE = mean(|y − ŷ|)
📐

RMSE

Root Mean Squared Error. Penalizes large errors more heavily than MAE. Lower is better.

RMSE = √mean((y − ŷ)²)
📉

MAPE

Mean Absolute Percentage Error. Expresses error as a percentage of actual price. <10% is excellent.

MAPE = mean(|y − ŷ| / y) × 100
